## The Problem

### 1. Circular Logic Trap
Twilio requires a "verifiable" opt-in mechanism but rejects the campaign because the opt-in isn't "functional" — we cannot make it functional without SMS capability, which requires an approved campaign. This is a catch-22 that forces repeated resubmissions at $15 each.

### 2. No Specific Rejection Feedback
The rejection provides only the generic error code 30896 with boilerplate "possible causes." No specific line item is flagged. No reviewer comments are provided. The business owner is left guessing what to fix, guaranteeing additional paid resubmissions.

### 3. Financial Impact
Each resubmission costs $15. Without specific feedback, the process becomes trial-and-error at the business owner's expense. For a small, sole-proprietor LLC, this is a material burden — especially when the documented requirements have been met.

### 4. All Documented Requirements Are Met
We have reviewed every item on Twilio's A2P 10DLC campaign approval best practices and CTIA Guidelines. Our submission includes:
- Detailed opt-in methods (verbal + web form)
- Live, clickable URLs for privacy policy and terms of service
- Explicit third-party sharing prohibition
- STOP opt-out in every message sample
- Clear business identification in all messages
- Consent language that meets TCPA requirements
